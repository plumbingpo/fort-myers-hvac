Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across Orlando

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: older gas furnaces on the older streets, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team works on every one of those systems. We have years of field experience, and we've learned how the local climate, the age of local homes, and local code affect the way a system runs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be straightforward: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Maintenance visits cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend equipment life.

For commercial property owners and managers nearby, we service the rooftop units, split systems, and dedicated ventilation equipment that restaurants, shops, offices, and apartment buildings depend on. Our commercial team understands that a hot dining room or a freezing office means lost business, so we prioritize fast diagnosis, stock common parts on the truck, and schedule work around your operating hours whenever we can.

If your heat or air quits, how fast help arrives matters more than anything else. For that reason, we keep our schedule flexible across the area and answer every call with a live person. Urgent calls typically see a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

Common emergencies we handle include air conditioners blowing warm air, outdoor units that hum but won't start, furnaces that ignite and then shut down, breakers that trip every time the system runs, frozen evaporator coils, clogged condensate drains backing up into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have stopped responding. We provide upfront pricing every time, so you know exactly what the work will cost before we begin.

Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or gives off a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. That small step can prevent a much larger repair.

How Orlando Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
